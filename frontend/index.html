The Problem & Solution

Flowers That Harm Can Heal the Planet

Every year, India alone generates over 800 million tonnes of flower waste from temples, religious events, and weddings. Most of this ends up in rivers or landfills, releasing toxic chemicals as it decomposes.

FloraCycle steps in at the source — collecting this waste and converting it into beautiful, sustainable products that support local livelihoods and protect our environment.
